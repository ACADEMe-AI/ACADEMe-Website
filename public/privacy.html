ACADEMe Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

ACADEMe ("we", "our", the "Platform") is an AI-assisted learning platform. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Minimum Age Requirement

The Platform is intended for users who are 13 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a user is under 13, we will delete their account and personal information.

Data We Collect

  • Account data: name, email address, profile photo (optional), class/grade level.
  • User content: photos and files you upload for courses, audio recordings you create within the app.
  • Learning data: course progress, quiz results, study time, AI chat conversations used to personalize learning.
  • Device data: basic analytics such as app version and crash reports.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and personalize the learning experience, including AI-generated recommendations.
  • To sync progress across devices and allow teachers to track enrolled students' progress.
  • To communicate transactional emails (verification codes, password resets).

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage & Security

Data is stored using Google Firebase / Google Cloud infrastructure with encryption in transit. Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing.

Your Rights: Access, Export & Deletion

You may delete your account at any time:

  • In-app: Profile → Delete account.
  • On the web (no app required): ACADEMe Account Deletion.

Deletion permanently removes your profile, progress, uploads, chat history and authentication credentials. For questions or data access requests, contact our support team.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with an updated date.
