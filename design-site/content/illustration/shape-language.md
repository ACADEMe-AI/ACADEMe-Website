# Shape language

Adapted from industry practice (e.g. Duolingo design.duolingo.com illustration guides). ACADEMe-owned colors and characters.

## Construction

**Only three primitives:**

1. Rounded rectangle (most used)  
2. Circle / ellipse  
3. Rounded triangle  

- All corners soft — **no sharp spikes**  
- Pathfinder OK if result stays soft  

## Simplicity budget

| Shape count | Verdict |
|-------------|---------|
| ~6 | Too abstract |
| **~12–18** | **Good** |
| ~30+ | Too busy |

## Rhythm

Vary size and placement; repeat one shape family for cohesion.

## Shadows

- Contact shadow = **pill** (rounded rect), not oval  
- Darker than the surface it sits on  
- Flat perspective (no heavy 3D vanishing)  

## Face kit

- Eyes geometric; prefer big readable eyes  
- Offset pupils for life  
- Mouth can be asymmetric  
- One signature feature (ribbon, tufts, etc.)
