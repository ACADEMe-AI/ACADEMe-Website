# Showcase website

The public site is one scroll film. The phone shows real app screens. The chapters below are the story as it is now.

## Hero

**Level up your learning.** With the cube **in your pocket.**

With AI, the way you study has to change. Adapt to it, or stay behind.

Button: **Start For Free.** Small line: No credit card required.

The hero does not list features. Each chapter below does one job.

## Chapters

1. **Put your material to work.** Phone: upload, then processing.
2. **A tutor that sees the context.** Ask the question in your own words. Pebby responds from the material you're actually studying. The app tab is ASKMe.
3. **In the language you think in.** English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, or Bengali. Pebby and your study cards use it. Switch any time.
4. **Turn review into recall.** Make flashcards, quiz yourself, and practise the ideas until you can retrieve them without looking.
5. **Personalized feedback.** Me, then My Progress. Where you're doing well, where to focus, and what to do next. The title is My Progress.
6. **Set the date.** A folder, the chapters, the test date. That becomes a plan, a Today list, and reminders.
7. **Join the community.** The Play listing is not public yet, so this button stays.

## Rules for this film
