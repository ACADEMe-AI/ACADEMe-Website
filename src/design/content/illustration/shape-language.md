# Shape

How Pebby is built, so every use stays on model.

## Body

- One rounded, slightly pear-shaped body, wider at the bottom
- A white rounded-rectangle face panel takes up the top half
- Three soft tufts on top, the middle one tallest
- Two short arms, two short rounded feet, a soft grey ground shadow
- Flat colour with a gentle top-light gradient (#A2A4FB → #8B8CF5 → #6E71D6). No outlines

## Face

- Two dark oval eyes with a small white highlight, eyebrows as short dashes
- A small mouth: a line, a smile or an open mouth, with a pink tongue when open
- Emotion comes from brows, eyes and mouth. The body shape stays the same

## Fit with the brand

- The rounded body echoes the app's 16 and 24 px radii
- Purple ties Pebby to `primary` (#564CF1) without matching it exactly, so buttons still stand out
- Pebby is flat; the cube is the only "3D" mark
