# USPs

Seven reasons a student picks ACADEMe, each with what proves it in the app today. From `tasks/strategy.md` §4. Describe other products only as "other study apps", never by name.

## 1. Knows your syllabus

Built for CBSE, ICSE and ISC, Class 6 to 12, from day one.

- 14 syllabus files for 2026-27, every chapter sourced to the board or publisher
- Courses shows every chapter for the student's class and board
- Pebby answers for your class and board

## 2. Learn by swiping

Lessons are 5 to 8 minute card decks, not long videos or notes.

- A lesson player with seven card types: ideas, tables, worked examples revealed a step at a time, summaries
- A quick check every few cards (+5 XP), with "here's why" when you miss
- Keep any card for revision. Pick up at the exact card you left

## 3. Pebby, a tutor with personality

The syllabus is the product; Pebby is the tutor inside it.

- ASKMe has three modes: Explain, Solve (hint first) and Quiz me
- Ask Pebby from any lesson card or scan
- Pebby is animated with dozens of poses and reacts as you go

## 4. Check my answer, board-style

Homework help that teaches how marks are given, not just the answer.

- Photograph a written answer and get marks by the CBSE or ICSE scheme
- Point by point: what earned marks, what's missing, how to get full marks, a model answer
- Solve gives a hint first and lets you take the next step

## 5. Your language

- Pebby answers in English, हिन्दी, తెలుగు, தமிழ் and বাংলা
- Exam terms stay in English in brackets
- App text and lessons in these languages come next

## 6. Fair

- No paywall while you set up
- Every lesson, revision, folder and reminder is free
- Only the costly AI is limited: 10 Pebby questions, 3 scans and 1 answer check a day on Free
- One simple Pro: ₹200 a month (first month ₹100) or ₹1,999 a year

## 7. Safe for students

- No ads, ever. No advertising ID
- Photos are never stored; only the text read from them
- Report on every Pebby answer
- Real account deletion, in the app and on the web, with a 30-day grace period
