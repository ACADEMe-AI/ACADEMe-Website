# Product

ACADEMe is a study app for Indian school students in Class 6 to 12: CBSE and ICSE, and ISC for Class 11 and 12, on the 2026-27 syllabus. Android first, on Google Play. There's no iOS app yet.

## Who it's for

The student. A 15-year-old in Class 10 with boards coming up, studying on a mid-range Android phone, often thinking in a language other than English. Parents pay for Pro, but there's no parent or teacher product.

## Five pillars

| Pillar | In the app |
|--------|------------|
| **Pebby tutor** | ASKMe: Explain · Solve (hint first) · Quiz me. Linked from every lesson card and every scan |
| **Swipe lessons from the syllabus** | Study → Courses: every chapter for your class and board, short card lessons with quick checks |
| **Scan with board-style marking** | The Scan key: Solve · Check my answer · Notes · Ask |
| **Folders and planner** | Study → Folders: a test date becomes a plan, a Today checklist and reminders |
| **Revision** | Kept cards and missed questions in one queue, plus chapter tests |

Streaks, XP, levels and the weekly report serve these five.

## Today, honestly

- Courses lists every chapter, but only **6 lessons are live** (Class 10 CBSE Maths and Science). The rest show "Coming soon". 5,777 lessons are planned
- Pebby answers in five languages; the app's menus are English for now
- Pebby and Check my answer need the AI service to have credits. When it doesn't, Pebby shows as unavailable
