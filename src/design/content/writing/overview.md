# Writing

How ACADEMe sounds in the app, on academe.cc, in the store listing and in emails.

## Pages

- [Voice](/design/writing/voice): tone, naming, celebrations, errors
- [Messaging](/design/writing/messaging): the lines we use and where

## Principles

1. Talk like a helpful older student, not a school office
2. Short and plain. One idea per sentence
3. Encourage without guilt. No "you'll lose your streak!"
4. Only say what the app really does today
5. Errors say what happened and what to do next
6. Never name another app or company. Say "other study apps" if you have to
