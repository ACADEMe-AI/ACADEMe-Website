## The five pillars, in plain words

1. **Pebby (ASKMe):** explain it simply, solve it with hints first, or quiz me
2. **Swipe lessons (Courses):** a few cards per lesson with a quick check every few cards
3. **Scan:** solve homework, check my answer the board's way, turn notes into a lesson, ask about a photo
4. **Folders and planner:** add chapters and a test date, get a plan, a Today list and reminders
5. **Revision:** kept cards and missed questions come back until you know them

## Pricing lines

- Free: every lesson, revision, folder and reminder. 10 Pebby questions, 3 scans and 1 answer check a day
- ACADEMe Pro: ₹200 a month, first month ₹100, or ₹1,999 a year
