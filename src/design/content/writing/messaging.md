# Messaging

## Elevator

ACADEMe is the study app for Class 6 to 12, CBSE, ICSE and ISC. The syllabus is the product. Pebby is the tutor inside it.

## Hero

> With AI, the way you study has to change. Adapt to it, or stay behind.

The headline above it stays: **Level up your learning. With the cube in your pocket.**

## What we do not put in the hero

Swipe lessons, Pebby, board-style marks, and class and board. Those show up as you scroll.
