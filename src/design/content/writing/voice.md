# Voice

## Tone

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Friendly, clear, encouraging | Baby talk, slang overload |
| "Nailed it." | Paragraphs of praise |
| "Couldn't read that photo. Try better light." | "Error 500" |
| "Here's why." | "Wrong!" |
| "Pebby gives you a hint first." | "Get instant answers" or anything that sounds like cheating |
| Indian examples: rupees, cricket, monsoon, local names | Examples that only make sense abroad |

## Naming

- **Pebby** is the tutor's name. Use it: "Ask Pebby", "Pebby answers in your language"
- **ACADEMe** always with a small e. **ACADEMe Pro** for the paid plan
- Tabs and features use the app's words: Home, ASKMe, Study, Me, Scan, Courses, Folders, Revision, Today, Check my answer, Explain, Solve, Quiz me
- Classes are "Class 10", boards are "CBSE", "ICSE" and "ISC"

## Celebrations

Six words or fewer. "Nailed it." · "Lesson done." · "+5 XP" · "Almost. One more." Celebrate the work, not the student's worth.

## Errors and limits

- Say what happened, then the next step: "You've used today's 10 Pebby questions. They're back at midnight, or go Pro for unlimited."
- Never blame the student
- When Pebby is unavailable, say so plainly and offer what still works (lessons, revision, folders)

## Honesty

- Pebby's answers come from AI and can be wrong. Say so where it matters
- Don't promise lessons for every chapter yet. Only a few are live; the rest are "Coming soon"
- No fake numbers, testimonials or "trusted by millions"

## Languages

Pebby answers in English, हिन्दी, తెలుగు, தமிழ் and বাংলা. Science and exam terms stay in English in brackets. The app's own menus are English for now, so say "Pebby answers in your language", not "the app is in your language".
