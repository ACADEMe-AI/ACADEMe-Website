# Voice

## Tone

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Sharp, encouraging, peer-like | Baby talk |
| “Nailed it.” | Paragraphs of praise |
| “Couldn’t read that photo — try better light” | “Error 500” only |
| Study smarter | Cheat framing |

## Mascot naming

- The name is **Pebby**, in the app and on the site.
- The tutor tab is **ASKMe**.
- Do not call Pebby Mee, and do not hide the name behind "learning buddy."

## Celebration copy

≤ 6 words. Examples: “Nailed it.” · “Still here. Let’s go.” · “Almost — one more.”
