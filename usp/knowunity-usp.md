## 3. AI Features (Full Breakdown)

### 3.1 AI Chat (`/ai/chat`)
- **Name**: Personal AI tutor / Knowunity AI
- **Tagline**: "Your personal AI tutor" / "Your study buddy"
- **Pitch**: Help with any subject, any time. Clear explanations, step-by-step problem solving, endless patience.
- **Sample prompts**: "Explain quantum physics", "Help with calculus", "French grammar tips"
- **Key benefits**:
  - Expert-level explanations
  - Available 24/7
  - Step-by-step guidance (don't just get the answer — understand the method)
  - Natural conversations (ask follow-ups, get clarification)
  - Multi-language support
  - Accurate & reliable (built on educational content verified by millions of students)

### 3.2 AI Flashcards (`/ai/flashcards`)
- **Tagline**: "Create smart flashcards instantly"
- **Pitch**: Upload any content and let AI generate study flashcards in seconds. Spaced repetition helps you actually remember it all.
- **Sample prompts**: "Create flashcards from my biology notes", "French Revolution", "Photosynthesis", "Spanish vocabulary"
- **Key benefits**:
  - Upload PDFs, images, or paste text — AI analyzes and pulls out key concepts
  - Generate hundreds of flashcards in seconds
  - Smart review with spaced repetition
  - Focus on weak spots — AI prioritizes cards you struggle with
  - Adaptive practice — cards adapt as you learn
  - Any subject supported

### 3.3 AI Quizzes (`/ai/quizzes`)
- **Tagline**: "Test your knowledge with smart quizzes"
- **Pitch**: Generate practice quizzes from any content in seconds. The AI adapts difficulty to your level.
- **Sample prompts**: "Quiz me on photosynthesis", "World War II", "Cell biology", "Algebra basics"
- **Key benefits**:
  - Multiple question types (multiple choice, true/false, fill-in-the-blank, and more)
  - AI-generated questions — sharp, relevant, often better than textbooks
  - Detailed analytics — track performance over time
  - Adapts to your level — starts easy, ramps up
  - Unlimited practice — fresh quizzes on any topic
  - Exam simulation — timed quizzes

### 3.4 AI Summary (`/ai/summary`)
- **Tagline**: "Summarize anything instantly"
- **Pitch**: Turn lengthy documents into concise summaries. AI pulls out key points so you can learn faster.
- **Sample prompts**: "Summarize chapter 3 on photosynthesis", "the American Revolution", "quantum mechanics", "climate change"
- **Key benefits**:
  - Summarize PDFs, articles, textbooks, lecture notes
  - Instant results — 50-page chapters into 5-minute reads
  - Key points extraction — picks out most important concepts, facts, definitions
  - Different summary lengths — quick overview or detailed breakdown
  - Study guide generation — organized with sections, headings, bullet points
  - Save hours of work

### 3.5 AI Practice Tests / Exam Prep (`/ai/exam-prep`)
- **Tagline**: "Ace your exams with practice tests"
- **Pitch**: Practice with realistic AI-generated exams. Detailed analytics, weak spot identification, and real confidence before the big day.
- **Sample exam**: "Biology practice test — 15 marks, 30 minutes"
- **Key benefits**:
  - Realistic practice tests that mirror real test formats
  - Timed practice sessions to build exam stamina
  - Performance analytics with detailed reports
  - Targeted review — AI spots weak areas and generates targeted questions
  - Full answer explanations — understand the why, not just the what
  - Score predictions based on practice results
