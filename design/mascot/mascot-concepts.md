All concepts are **AI concept sketches** for decision-making, not final production art.

| ID | Name | Metaphor | Why it might work | Risk |
|----|------|----------|-------------------|------|
| **R2-1** | **Pip** the desk cactus | Desk plant that thrives when you study | Adult desk energy; growth = streaks; not “AI animal” | Can feel cute-plant app |
| **R2-2** | **Fold** the paper plane | Notes that take flight | Study → momentum; light, quick, memorable silhouette | Paper-plane apps exist; keep distinctive face/style |
| **R2-3** | **Lumen** the study lantern | Late-night lamp companion | Perfect for night study; warm + our cool blue | Might feel cozy/slow vs “fast TikTok” |

- Would a 21-year-old screenshot this without cringe?  
- Distinct from Duo owl + KnowUnity fox?  
- Readable at 64px?  
- Works on light **and** dark?  
- Can Rive animate it without 50 layers of fur?
