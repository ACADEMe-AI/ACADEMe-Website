## How to judge (from research)

Score each 1–5:

1. **Personality clear** without reading a label?  
2. **Silhouette** unique at small size?  
3. **Signature feature** one thing?  
4. **Fits college peer** (not baby / not Duo guilt)?  
5. **≠ KnowUnity fox / Duo owl?**  
6. **Animatable** with our state catalog?  
7. **Exam-complete moment** feel right (Freddie rule)?
